Reduce re-quotes in quote-to-close

Opportunity owner: VP Revenue Operations
Salesforce → event log → Apromore
Time frame: Filter 2: New business opportunities Filter 3: Deals that reached Negotiation

Challenge

    Root cause illustrative · reason codes to be captured

    • Re-quote reasons to break down: quote expired while awaiting approval, discount renegotiated, scope change, pricing error.
    • Hotspots by approver queue, region and rep: approvals handled, median wait, re-quote rate. EMEA's single approval queue is the first suspect.
    • Causal chain to confirm: slow approval → quote expires → re-quote → lower win rate.

    Solution

    • Apromore process discovery and performance views expose where quotes wait and loop back.
    • Conformance checking flags deals that skip Qualification or bypass approval steps.
    • Simulate a delegated EMEA approval threshold (discounts up to 30%) before changing policy, then monitor it.

    Tracking

    P&L impact: revenue (win rate)
