Reduce re-quotes in quote-to-close

Proposed opportunity owner: VP Revenue Operations
Salesforce data → event log → process intelligence
Time frame: Data: simulated, Salesforce object shape Filter 3: Deals that reached Negotiation

Challenge

    Root cause illustrative · reason codes to be captured

    • Re-quote reasons to break down: quote expired while awaiting approval, discount renegotiated, scope change, pricing error.
    • Hotspots by approver queue, region and rep: approvals handled, median wait, re-quote rate. EMEA's single approval queue is the first suspect.
    • Test whether approval delay causes quote expiry and re-quotes before acting on it.

    Solution

    • Process discovery and performance views expose where quotes wait and loop back.
    • Conformance checking flags deals that skip Qualification or bypass approval steps.
    • Agree a controlled pilot with RevOps and Finance once re-quote causes are validated.

    Tracking

    P&L impact: revenue (win rate)
    (Baseline re-quote rate − re-quote rate after intervention) × negotiated deals per year × win-rate gap × average won deal
    Baseline: of negotiated deals re-quoted (frozen)
    After intervention: TBD, measured against a comparison group
    Realized revenue: validated by Finance, not modelled
