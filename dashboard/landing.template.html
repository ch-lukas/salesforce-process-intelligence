Business case · deep-dive on item 2

Reduce re-quotes in quote-to-close

Proposed opportunity owner: VP Revenue Operations.

Time frame: Scope: deals that reached Negotiation Data: simulated, Salesforce object shape

Challenge

    Root cause to capture

    • Re-quote reasons to break down: quote expired while awaiting approval, discount renegotiated, scope change, pricing error.
    • Hotspots by approver queue, region and rep: approvals handled, median wait, re-quote rate.
    • Test whether approval delay causes quote expiry and re-quotes before acting on it.

    Solution

    • Process discovery and performance views expose where quotes wait and loop back.
    • Conformance checking flags deals that skip Qualification or bypass approval steps.
    • Agree a controlled pilot with RevOps and Finance once re-quote causes are validated.

    Tracking

    (Baseline re-quote share − share after intervention) × negotiated deals per year × win-rate gap × average won deal
    • Baseline: of negotiated deals re-quoted (frozen)
    • After intervention: measured against a comparison group
    • Realized revenue: validated by Finance, not modelled

    Salesforce data to Process Intelligence in four steps

    The same pipeline, run against a real Salesforce trial org. It proves the plumbing works; the business numbers above come from the simulated dataset.

    1. ExtractSOQL over the REST API for Lead, Opportunity, OpportunityHistory and Task, signed by the Salesforce CLI session. extract.py
    2. CorrelateOne case per deal: Opportunity Id. Tasks join on WhatId, leads on ConvertedOpportunityId. transform.py
    3. LoadCSV and IEEE XES with activity, timestamps, resource and case attributes, ready to import into a process mining tool.
    4. Mine and sizeDirectly-follows graph, variants, cohort win rates and a driver-based value model. mine.py
